Metro Detroit APA runs American Poolplayers Association (APA) leagues and tournaments in Macomb, Oakland, Washtenaw and Wayne counties. We are an independently owned APA franchise. This policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and how our text messages work.

Information we collect

  • Your name and APA member number
  • Your mobile phone number and email address
  • League and tournament details, such as your skill level, the events you enter and your match results

We collect this when you join a team, register for an event, or check in at a tournament.

How we use it

  • To run our leagues and tournaments: schedules, brackets, table assignments and results
  • To contact you about league business and events you have entered
  • To send text messages, only if you have agreed to receive them (see below)

Player names and results may appear on tournament brackets and results pages we publish for the event. Phone numbers and email addresses are never shown on them.

Text messages

  • What you'll receive: League updates and messages about tournaments you have entered, such as match calls, table assignments, cancelled calls, results and links to the live bracket.
  • How you agree: by selecting the optional text-message question when you register for a tournament, or on our Contact List Opt-In form. You don't need to agree to register or play.
  • How often: message frequency varies.
  • Cost: message and data rates may apply.
  • To stop: reply STOP to any message. We'll confirm, and you won't get any more texts unless you agree again.
  • For help: reply HELP, or email us at metrodetroitapa@gmail.com.
  • Carriers are not liable for delayed or undelivered messages.

We do not sell or share your SMS opt-in data or personal information with third parties for marketing purposes.

No mobile information will be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes.

Text messaging opt-in data and consent will not be shared with, sold to, or transferred to any third party, affiliate or lead generator.

How we share information

We do not sell your personal information. We share it only:

  • with the American Poolplayers Association, for league membership, records and national events
  • with the service providers that help us run events, such as our text message provider and our online forms, who may use it only to provide those services to us
  • when the law requires it

None of this sharing includes your text messaging opt-in or consent.

Keeping your information

We keep your information while you are an active member or player, and as long as needed for league records. We take reasonable steps to protect it.

Your choices

To update or remove your information, or to stop texts, email us at metrodetroitapa@gmail.com or reply STOP to any text.

Changes

We may update this policy. The "Last updated" date above shows when it last changed.
