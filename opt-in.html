Text Message Opt-In

Metro Detroit APA

Players and members opt in to Metro Detroit APA text messages on either of two web forms.

1. Tournament registration form

Players register for each tournament on this Google Form: tournament registration form. It opens for each event's registration window and is closed between events, so the consent question is shown below as it appears on the form.

After entering their mobile number, players see an optional "Text Message Updates" section with an unchecked "I agree" checkbox. Checking it is not required to register.

2. Contact List Opt-In form

Members can opt in at any time on our Contact List Opt-In form, which asks for name, email and mobile number and requires checking "Yes, please opt me into all text messages from Metro Detroit APA."
